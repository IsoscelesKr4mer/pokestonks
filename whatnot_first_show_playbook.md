## Part 2 — Energy break mechanics (so you can write your rules)

### Spot structure

Modern Pokemon TCG has 11 basic energy types but the standard energy break uses **8 spots**:

1. **Grass** (G)
2. **Fire** (R)
3. **Water** (W)
4. **Lightning / Electric** (L)
5. **Psychic** (P)
6. **Fighting** (F)
7. **Darkness** (D)
8. **Metal** (M)

Dragon, Fairy, and Colorless are usually rolled into a randomizer rule (see below). Most break runners on Whatnot use 8 spots — buyers expect it.

### The "energy card in pack" rule (the standard)

- Every modern Pokemon booster pack contains 1 basic energy card, randomized.
- Dealer pulls the basic energy card out of each pack and reveals it on camera.
- The energy type of that card decides who wins the **entire pack** (all 10 cards including any hits).
- Because the energy is randomized at packaging, **chase cards distribute randomly across spots** — a Mega Charizard X SIR or Pikachu ex SIR could land in ANY spot depending on what energy was packed with it.
- This is what makes the format clean: all 8 spots have equal expected value, so all 8 spots cost the same.

### Required published rules (Whatnot policy)

Whatnot's Card Breaks Policy requires you to publish in the listing description AND show notes:
- Exact spot count and what each spot wins
- Whether bulk (commons/uncommons) is shipped or kept
- Tiebreaker / randomizer rules for trainers and unmatched cards
- What happens if a spot's energy doesn't appear (sometimes called "hot potato" — usually they get one bonus pack at the end)

### Rules template (paste into your Whatnot listing)

> **Perfect Order 18-Pack Energy Break — Rules**
>
> 8 energy spots: Grass, Fire, Water, Lightning, Psychic, Fighting, Darkness, Metal.
>
> **How packs are awarded:** I pull the basic energy card from each pack and reveal it on camera. The energy type of that card determines which spot wins the whole pack (all 10 cards, including any hits).
>
> **Special Energy or non-basic energy as the energy card:** randomized to one of the 8 spots using Whatnot's randomizer wheel, on stream. (This is rare — packs almost always contain a basic energy.)
>
> **Hits and Special Illustration Rares are awarded to the spot that wins the pack they came in.** No exceptions, no separate randomizer for hits.
>
> **Bulk (commons / uncommons / energy cards) IS shipped** to each spot owner — every card in your won packs is yours.
>
> **Code cards** are NOT shipped (industry standard). I keep them or burn them.
>
> **If your energy never appears as a top card** in 18 packs, you receive 1 bonus pack at the end, randomly selected from my next-set inventory.
>
> **Shipping:** Free PWE for orders under $20 of value, $5 bubble mailer with tracking otherwise. Combined shipping for multiple wins.

This is a clean ruleset that buyers immediately understand and that protects you from disputes.

### Whatnot fees (May 2026, US seller)

Per [official Whatnot seller fees page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4847069165965-Whatnot-seller-fees), in the "US, Canada, and Australia" table for **Comics, TCG, Sports Singles, Toys & Hobbies**:

- **Commission: 8% on item price up to $1,500** (0% on portion above $1,500 — that's the "limited time" promo, only matters for high-ticket singles)
- **Payment processing: 2.9% on total order value (item + shipping + tax) + $0.30 per transaction**
- **All-in on a $25 spot: ~12%**

**Critical:** commission applies to item price only. Shipping fees you charge separately are not hit by the 8% — they only pay the 2.9% + $0.30 processing fee.

(The 4% rate you may have seen elsewhere applies to **Coins & Money**, not TCG. The 4% Australia promo is for AU-based sellers only.)

## Part 4 — Run-of-show playbook

### Pre-show checklist (the day of)

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| **24 hours before** | Schedule the show in Whatnot. Title: "Perfect Order 18-Pack Energy Break · 8 Spots · First Show!" Include "First Show" hashtag — buyers love a first-show discount energy. |
| **24 hours before** | Pre-build the break in Break Manager: 8 spots, each named after an energy type, $25 each. Pin it to the show. |
| **24 hours before** | Promote: Instagram story, X post, any local Pokemon Discord, the message in your store URL. Specifically: "First Whatnot show Friday 8pm Pacific. PO half-box energy break, $25/spot." |
| **2 hours before** | Charge phone to 100%, plug into wall during show. Test ring light. Test WiFi speed (>15 Mbps up minimum). |
| **30 minutes before** | Lay out: the 18 packs (do NOT pre-open), penny sleeves, toploaders, team bags, painter's tape, sharpie, spot board with 8 energies listed. |
| **15 minutes before** | Open Whatnot, go to your show, check camera framing. Test lighting on your hands. |
| **5 minutes before** | Go live. Read your rules aloud while the first viewers join. Don't open anything yet. |

### Opening script (90-120 seconds, scripted)

> "Hey everyone, welcome in. I'm Michael, this is my first show on Whatnot. I'm running a Perfect Order 18-pack energy break tonight — 8 spots, $25 each, every card in your won packs goes to you including bulk, hits stay with the pack they came in.
>
> Rules are pinned to the show — please give them a quick read. Trainers and unmatched cards go to the randomizer wheel right here on screen. If your energy never shows up, you get a bonus pack from my next show.
>
> If you're new to me, I source sealed Pokemon out of Washington state, ship anywhere in the US, and I'm running the show out of my own collection. I've got Prismatic Evolutions, White Flare, Mega Evolution, and a bunch more coming up in future shows — drop a follow if you want a heads up.
>
> I'm going to give the spots a couple minutes to fill. While we wait, ask me anything in chat."

### "Workable but stiff" tactics

You said you're not a natural on camera. Here's how to compensate:

- **Pre-write 5 transition lines** on a sticky note out of frame. Examples:
  - "Alright, into the next pack."
  - "Big one for [energy]. Let's see what's in there."
  - "Bulk for this one but those PO commons are clean."
  - "Trainer top card — to the wheel."
  - "Reverse holo, nice. That's still a $1-2 card on TCGplayer."
- **Don't try to be hype.** Be calm and accurate. Buyers respect a seller who says "this is a $4 hit" instead of fake-screaming over a reverse holo.
- **Read every chat message aloud.** Even a "hi" gets "hey [username], welcome in." This is the single highest-leverage habit for stiff-on-cam sellers. It fills dead air, makes viewers feel seen, and gets the algorithm boost.
- **Have a kitchen timer or phone timer** going off every 15 minutes, set silent — when it buzzes, you do a quick "thanks for being in chat, here's what's coming up next" beat.
- **Slow > fast.** Stiff-and-slow reads as "trustworthy seller." Stiff-and-trying-to-be-fast reads as "panicked." Lean into your natural pace.

### The actual break flow (rinse and repeat 18 times)

For each pack:

1. Hold pack to camera, show the wrapper (and that it's sealed). 2-3 seconds.
2. Open from the bottom-back to preserve the wrapper's sealed look on the front. Slide cards out.
3. Find the basic energy card (usually near the back of the pack), reveal it face-up to camera. Read the energy type aloud: "Lightning energy. That pack goes to [username on Lightning spot]."
4. Fan the rest of the pack to camera, calling out anything notable: "Reverse holo Larry, Mega Charizard ex line, Nest Ball — solid pack." If there's a hit, build the moment: "Big SIR for Lightning, congrats [username]."
5. Drop the entire pack into a labeled team bag pre-marked with the energy spot owner's username (have 8 team bags pre-labeled before show start).
6. Move to next pack within 30-45 seconds.

Total break time for 18 packs: ~15-20 minutes of pure ripping, plus chat engagement = 30-40 minutes total.

### What to do if spots don't fill

This will happen show #1. Plan for it.

- **Fill window:** give it 10-15 minutes after going live.
- **If 6+ spots fill:** start the break, keep last 2 spots open for live bidders. Sometimes they jump in mid-rip.
- **If <6 spots fill:** drop the floor by $3-5 ("first-show special, last 4 spots at $20"). Better to clear at break-even than not run the show.
- **If <4 spots fill after price drop:** pivot to personal pack rips. Auction packs individually starting at $5, let them go up. You preserve cost basis and learn the auction interface. Mention that the break is rescheduled.

### Closing script (after last pack)

> "That's the break. Big winners tonight: [username] on [energy] pulled [card]. Thanks for jumping in, especially first-time. I'll be back [day, time] with a full 36-pack PO box break. Drop a follow if you want a notification.
>
> Shipping goes out [tomorrow / Monday]. PWE under $20 of value, bubble mailer with tracking over. Combined shipping if you won multiple spots. You'll get tracking in your Whatnot DMs.
>
> Thanks everyone. Goodnight."

### Post-show shipping (within 48 hours)

1. Whatnot generates labels in the seller dashboard. Print them on whatever printer you have.
2. For each spot owner: pull their team bag of packs, sort the contents:
   - Hits (rares, holos, ex, full art): penny sleeve → toploader → team bag → painter's tape seal
   - Bulk: rubber band or stack in a bag
3. PWE for under-$20 shipments: cardboard sandwich + toploader, fit in #10 envelope, $1.65 stamp + non-machinable surcharge. Mark "Non-Machinable" on the envelope.
4. Bubble mailer for over-$20: pack flat, stick label, drop at USPS or schedule pickup.
5. Mark shipped in Whatnot dashboard. This triggers buyer release of payment.

## Part 5 — Common pitfalls (avoid these specifically)

| Pitfall | What happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Not pinning rules in show notes | Buyers complain mid-show, demand refunds, you lose rep | Pin rules before going live, read aloud at start |
| Going live without practice handle of the Whatnot interface | You fumble the randomizer button live, energy goes wrong, you eat a refund | Run a 5-minute test stream Wed evening with no break — just talk to nobody to learn buttons |
| Pricing spots too high "because Mega Zygarde is $170" | Spots don't fill, show dies | Price for the AVERAGE box, not the dream box. Hits are 1-in-6 odds. |
| Forgetting Whatnot fees in your math | You "made" $30 profit that's actually $5 after fees | Always calculate at 12% all-in for sub-$50 spots |
| Going live during a major Whatnot Pokemon event | Your audience gets siphoned by a 50k-follower seller | Check Whatnot home for major scheduled events; pick a quieter slot |
| Doing the show on cellular | Wifi drops mid-rip, refunds | Hardwire router, kill the dishwasher, no streaming TV in another room |
| Running a 36-pack break show #1 | You panic at pack 14, make a mistake, it costs you | 18-pack first. Lower stakes, faster feedback loop |
| Trying to talk constantly | You burn out by pack 8 | It's OK to be quiet for 5-10 seconds while you flip a card. Calm = competent. |
| Skipping post-show shipping for 4 days | Buyers leave bad reviews | Ship within 48 hours, every time |

## Part 6 — When to graduate to bigger stakes

Track these metrics across your first 5 shows:

- **Show 1 goal:** complete the show without a meltdown, ship everything in 48h, get 30+ followers
- **Show 2 goal:** clear 20%+ margin on a full box break
- **Show 3 goal:** 50+ live viewers at peak, run a Prismatic Evolutions break with $50+ spots
- **Show 4 goal:** 100 followers, run a multi-set themed break ("Mega Block Sampler")
- **Show 5 goal:** Develop one signature format buyers come back for (e.g., "Friday 8pm energy breaks")

Once you've got 100+ followers and 5+ clean shows, you can start running:
- Character breaks (more setup but higher per-spot pricing)
- Combo breaks (e.g., 6-pack White Flare + 6-pack Black Bolt = full Pikachu/Eternatus break)
- Auction-only shows (rip & ship + selling singles)
