**Two card-number collisions were found and both are now resolved** (`scripts/fix-card-misreads-0820.ts`). Neither came from this drop; all the implicated rows predate it.

**2026 Topps Chrome #7 -> Basallo keeps 7, Ohtani moved to 1.** Both Basallo backs print 7 plainly. The Ohtani back prints **1**: cropped, rotated upright and upscaled 6x it is an unmistakable 1 above CHROME, with the flag serif and no crossbar. **This is the third recorded instance of an Ohtani logged as 7 when it is 1** — the card-intake skill already documents two. Worth treating "Ohtani #7" as a standing smell.
- The live eBay listing (#168555750100, $99.99) carried #7 in both its **title and description**. Both fixed. The title lives on the inventory item, the description on the offer; updating only the inventory item leaves the wrong number visible to buyers.

**2026 Topps Finest #9 -> Crawford keeps 9, Murakami is #5.** Crawford's back is an unmistakable 9 (round bowl, dot counter, tail descending left), so he keeps it. Murakami's glyph is visibly different, so he was certainly not 9. I read it at 14x as an **8** and set it to 8 with a `confirm card number` flag, because Finest's stylised numerals are not safe to call off a blurry crop. **The flag was right and the reading was wrong.** The checklist settles it:

| # | player |
|---|---|
| 5 | Munetaka Murakami, Chicago White Sox RC |
| 8 | Jose Altuve, Houston Astros |
| 9 | Justin Crawford, Philadelphia Phillies RC |

Two independent checklists agree (cardsmithsbreaks, checklistinsider) and the back reads CHICAGO WHITE SOX - 1B with the RC badge, matching #5 exactly. The stylised Finest **5** is what read as an 8. Corrected by `scripts/fix-murakami-cardnum-0820.ts`.

**Lesson: do not resolve a card number by staring harder at a blurry glyph.** Three passes of zooming produced two wrong answers; one checklist fetch produced the right one in seconds. Zoom to read a marker that is definitely there (the REFRACTOR band); use the checklist to resolve a number that is in doubt.

### Two defects the dry run caught, both mine

**The ingest dropped the RC flag.** Variation labels take RC from `/RC/` in `notes`, and my ingest wrote only the rip string, so all 27 rookies would have listed without RC. Worse, three cards already on the listing *with* RC would not have matched and would have been added a second time. Fixed by rewriting the notes from the saved read before merging. **If an ingest feeds a label builder, it has to write every field that builder reads.**

**Matching on the whole label was wrong twice over.** Full-string matching missed cards whose live label differed only by RC. Relaxing the key to `<number> - <player>` then over-matched: it produced 23 bumps including cards whose live variation is a *different parallel*, which would have put X-Fractor stock onto a Base entry. The right key strips only a trailing " RC" and keeps the parallel. That took it back to the correct **13 bumps + 35 new**.
