# Kayou Naruto reference

Kayou rebuilds its rarity ladder **per set**. A code that means "top chase" in one
set can be mid in another, and there is no universal Kayou rarity chart. Always
identify the set first, then look up that set's ladder. Never carry a ladder over.

Read the set off the card code, not the box: `NREA02-CR-003` = **N**aruto **E**arth
Scroll series **02**, rarity CR, card 003.

## Earth Scroll Series 2 (NREA02) — the set Michael owns

Bought 2026-08-19 at Target Northgate, 6 boxes at $11.05 (ci135082). Box model
NR-KP-DZJLH-002-5P-NA, 5 packs x 7 cards. Same card pool and same published odds
as the 20-pack booster box (NR-KP-DZJ-002A-NA); only the pack count differs.

132 cards across 8 tiers:

| Tier | Name | In set | Art style | Active-ask median |
|------|------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| CR | Creative Rare | 4 | Scroll of Ninja | $75, top card $400+ |
| AR | Amber Rare | 10 | ukiyo-e battle scenes | $52, top card $250+ |
| MR | Miracle Rare | 4 | tarot layout | $10-60 by character |
| ◇UR | Parallel/Diamond UR | 4 | numbered UR-001..004 | $21-45 |
| UR | Ultra Rare | 15 | pop art, UR-005..015 | **$3-5** |
| SSR | Super Spark Rare | 25 | - | $2 |
| SR | Super Rare | 20 | - | bulk |
| R | Rare | 50 | - | bulk |

**The tier code alone does not price a card.** Two traps, both hit on 2026-08-27:

- `UR-001` through `UR-004` are the **Diamond parallels** at $21-45. `UR-005`
  and up are plain URs at $3-5. Same two letters, ten times the money.
- MR spans $10 (MR-001 Kabuto, The Moon) to $60 (MR-002 Tsunade, Justice).

A wide tier query also drags in Diamond parallels, graded slabs and multi-card
lots, which is how a $3 card first got quoted at $10 and a $10 card at $35.
Query wide, then filter the RESULTS on the full card number, and exclude
graded/lot titles before taking a median.

### The four MRs (tarot subset)

| Code | Character | Tarot | Raw ask |
|------|-----------|-------|---------|
| MR-001 | Yakushi Kabuto | The Moon | ~$10 |
| MR-002 | Tsunade | Justice | ~$60 |
| MR-003 | Itachi Uchiha | The Hermit | ~$32-52 |
| MR-004 | - | The Hierophant | - |

**UR outranks SSR.** Backwards from most anime TCGs; more S's is not better.

The two cards that carry the set:
- **Itachi Uchiha NREA02-CR-003** — $400-500 asks
- **Naruto Uzumaki NREA02-AR-008** — $250-275 asks

Codes often carry an `L3`/`L4`/`L5` suffix. Observed L5 on CR, L4 on AR, L3 on
UR/SSR, so it appears to track tier. That is read off eBay listings, NOT off
Kayou's own page, so treat it as a hint and confirm against the rarity code.

### Published pull rates (Kayou official, per pack)

Every pack is `3R + 2SR + 1SSR` plus **one hit slot**, and the hit slot is 50/50:

- 50%: `1 UR`
- 50%: `1 of SSR / ◇UR / AR / MR / CR`

So a 5-pack collector box has exactly **5 cards that can matter**, and roughly
half of those land as a plain ~$10 UR. Everything above UR comes out of the other
branch, where SSR is the most common outcome.

Prices above are Browse API **active asks** scanned 2026-08-27, not solds. Read
them as a ceiling. Rescan before pricing anything real.

## Rip vs sell sealed

Measured on the box opened 2026-08-27 (15R, 10SR, 7SSR, UR-005, UR-014, MR-001):

| | Asks | Net after fees | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rip and sell singles | ~$26 | ~$16 | 4 listings, 4 packages |
| Sell the box sealed | $13-19 | ~$13 | 1 listing, 1 package |

Ripping cleared about **$3 more** than selling sealed, for four times the work.
At a $11.05 cost and a $15 sealed market, **sell sealed** is the default; the
CR/AR lottery is the only argument for ripping and Kayou does not publish the
distribution inside the hit pool, so do not compute an EV for it.
